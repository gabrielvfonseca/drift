# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use the library and for the community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to gabriel@frontal.dev. Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially.
The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that
break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for serious or repeated violations.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
